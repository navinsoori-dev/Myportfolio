YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This notice explains how VNS DIGITAL handles information submitted through the project enquiry form on this website. Please do not submit passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information.

Who receives your enquiry?

VNS DIGITAL is an independent automation studio based in Rome, Italy. For privacy questions or requests about an enquiry, contact vnsdigital.rome@gmail.com.

What information is collected?

If you submit the enquiry form, we collect your name, email address, optional company or website, the service you are interested in, and any message you choose to provide. Basic technical request information may also be processed by the hosting and security infrastructure.

Why is it used?

The information is used to review and respond to your request, communicate about a potential project, and protect the enquiry form from abuse. We do not sell enquiry information or use it for unrelated advertising.

Where is it stored?

Enquiry records are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database configured with an EU jurisdiction. The website and API are hosted on Cloudflare infrastructure. Infrastructure providers process data to deliver and secure the service.

How long is it kept?

Enquiries are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request and manage related follow-up, unless a longer period is required by law. A deletion request can be sent to the contact email above.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable data protection law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, or object to or restrict certain processing. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority. Contact us by email to make a request.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the website and its services change. The latest version will be published on this page.

This is a basic website privacy notice and should be reviewed by a qualified privacy professional before the business begins collecting enquiries at scale.
